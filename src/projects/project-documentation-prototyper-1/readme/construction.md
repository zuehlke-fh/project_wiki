# Construction: [LockQuest]

## Description
The construction is split in two parts:
- Electronic part
- Woodcrafting part

## Content
### Electronic part
The core of the circuit is an esp32 and it connects to all other electronic components.\
Pin A3 and A4 are connected to hand trainers with pull-down resistors.\
Pin A5 is connected to the sound module (buzzer).\
Pin 8 is connected to the servo.\
SCL and SDA are connected to the gyro sensor.
![Image example](/projects/project-documentation-prototyper-1/images/image8.jpg)
![Image example](/projects/project-documentation-prototyper-1/images/image5.jpg)


### Woodcrafting part
The chest is designed with a double floor, to hide the electronic parts.
![Image example](/projects/project-documentation-prototyper-1/images/image3.jpg)
Under the double floor is space for the breadboard with the circuit and a powerbank.
![Image example](/projects/project-documentation-prototyper-1/images/image2.jpg)
On the outside there are two hinges to open and close the chest easier and on the sides there are handles for better transportation.
![Image example](/projects/project-documentation-prototyper-1/images/image4.jpg)
